Araye Modern was founded on a single conviction: that industrial and marine projects deserve an engineering partner who can carry the work from the first drawing to the final inspection without handing it to someone else.

What began as a precision machining workshop grew into an integrated engineering and manufacturing operation: digital engineering and 3D technology, precision machining, industrial fabrication and systems, inspection and NDT, automation and control, and marine technology with subsea robotics.

Today we serve industrial, marine and offshore clients with the same principle — understand the requirement first, choose the right technical approach, and produce parts and systems that can be relied on for the service life they were designed for.

Eng. Malek Mazarei
